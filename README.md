# MathScanner
A simple iOS app to calculate math. Features include: No Typing Required - The app scans LIVE while you hover your camera over math, Privacy Based - We do not sell your soul to third partys, No Ads or Daily Limit - Yea none And this app is FREE, Everything happens locally - Happens all on your device, iOS 14/13 to the newest.
